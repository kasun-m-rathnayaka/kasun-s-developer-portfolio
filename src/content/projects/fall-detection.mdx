## Problem

Falls among elderly people living alone often go unnoticed for too long.

## What I built

A CCTV monitoring system that tracks human pose in real time with YOLO and MediaPipe, detects falls and unusual behaviour, and serves alerts through FastAPI.

## Outcome

Falls and unusual behaviour are flagged in real time from CCTV, so incidents involving people living alone can be noticed and acted on quickly instead of going unnoticed.
